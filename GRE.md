
# Test Structure

Total Time: ~1 hour 58 minutes. Total Questions: 54 multiple-choice + 1 essay.

| Section                  | Questions | Time Limit | Average Time / Question |
| ------------------------ | --------- | ---------- | ----------------------- |
| Analytical Writing (AWA) | 1 Essay   | 30 minutes | 30:00                   |
| Verbal Reasoning 1       | 12        | 18 minutes | 1:30                    |
| Verbal Reasoning 2       | 15        | 23 minutes | 1:32                    |
| Quantitative Reasoning 1 | 12        | 21 minutes | 1:45                    |
| Quantitative Reasoning 2 | 15        | 26 minutes | 1:44                    |
- Analytical Writing:  
    Analyze an Issue: You will evaluate a claim and write a well-reasoned argument supporting your perspective.
- Verbal Reasoning:  
    - Text Completion: Fill in 1, 2, or 3 blanks within a short passage.  
    - Sentence Equivalence: Select two words that complete a sentence and give it the same meaning.  
    - Reading Comprehension: Includes single-choice, multiple-choice (where one or more answers can be correct), and select-in-passage (clicking a sentence in the text that meets a specific criteria).
- Quantitative Reasoning:  
    - Quantitative Comparison (QC): Compare Quantity A and Quantity B and determine their mathematical relationship.  
    - Multiple-Choice (Single Answer): Standard math problem with one correct option.  
    - Multiple-Choice (One or More Answers): Select all choices that apply.  
    - Numeric Entry: Type the exact numerical answer into a box (no choices provided).  
    - Data Interpretation (DI): Sets of questions based on a provided graph, table, scatterplot, or chart.
